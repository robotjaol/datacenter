# Investment memo — illustrative facility case

**Decision:** retain the reference as a transparent concept case and resolve commercial/site evidence before any investment recommendation. The base assumptions yield a slightly negative NPV; the model does not manufacture a positive business case.

| Metric | Reference result | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Design IT demand | 5.00 MW | Four halls × 50 racks × 25 kW |
| Design facility demand | 6.814 MW | Full design IT at 35 C, before sizing margin |
| Annual facility energy | 44.242 GWh | Fully leased, 75% average draw, synthetic year |
| Annual PUE | 1.347 | Ratio of annual energies |
| Initial CAPEX | USD 57.60 million | USD 48m allowance plus 20% contingency |
| Pre-tax unlevered NPV | USD -0.329 million | 10% nominal discount, 15 years |
| Conventional IRR | 9.92% | Nominal pre-tax facility cash flow |
| Simple payback | 8.67 years | Undiscounted first crossing |

Capacity pricing starts at USD 180/kW-month; annual occupancy ramps from 35% to 95%. Power uses the synthetic USD 0.10/kWh tariff plus USD 5/kW-month demand charge. These are author assumptions, not market quotes. Energy reimbursement is 100% in the base case and is shown separately from cost. Annual headline energy is not year-one occupied consumption.

The most useful next evidence is site/grid deliverability, actual equipment/contractor quotes, lease and energy-recovery terms, construction phasing and verified operating curves. A nine-case sensitivity table shows how price and base capital change value. No debt, tax, financing fees, working capital or construction delay is modeled.

The residual asset allowance is 15% of base CAPEX, escalated to final year. Test zero residual and delayed occupancy before drawing a commercial conclusion. See the [methodology](../docs/model-methodology.md) and [source register](../research/source-register.md).
