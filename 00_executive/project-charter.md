# Project charter

**Sponsor/author:** Jonathan Oktaviano. **Purpose:** demonstrate integrated facility engineering judgment in a public portfolio. **Reference:** hypothetical tropical Indonesia facility; no land, customer or utility commitment.

Success means another reviewer can change a scenario, run calculations, inspect source assumptions, reconcile equipment and energy totals, and trace requirements to evidence. Quality is measured by reproducibility and transparent boundaries, not file count.

The initial case provides 5 MW design IT in four equal halls, with a 70/30 air/liquid IT-heat split. Electrical A/B paths each receive a full-load allowance. Cooling uses a shared N+1 plant and concept isolation sections; concurrent maintainability is an objective to verify.

Scope includes site screening, shell/space allowances, power, cooling, energy, facility economics, supervisory controls, asset/twin contracts, reliability, commissioning, operations and delivery governance. Compute hardware, applications, cloud services and hyperscaler proprietary specifications are excluded.

Release acceptance requires passing numerical tests; traceable synthetic inputs; usable diagrams and workbook; a source/license register; and an explicit remaining-engineering register. Any physical project would require owner approval, qualified designers and authority review at the relevant gates.
