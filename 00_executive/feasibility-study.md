# Concept feasibility

The facility is computationally consistent at 5 MW IT. It is not established as commercially or physically feasible at a real site. Site Alpha ranks first among synthetic eligible candidates; Site Beta is excluded by the illustrative hazard gate despite a strong weighted score.

The critical dependencies are deliverable electrical capacity, independent path routing, cooling ride-through, protected space, flood and seismic evidence, and facility lease economics. Equipment nameplates are allowances, not purchase selections. A/B capacity does not eliminate the common grid, shared cooling, controls, fuel or human-error risks.

Decision alternatives: phased commissioning versus all-at-once capital; air-only versus mixed liquid cooling; two dedicated electrical paths versus alternate topology; and asset residual value versus zero terminal recovery. Use sensitivity results and the FMEA before increasing redundancy or claiming economic benefit.

Advance to a real-site feasibility gate only after obtaining a utility connection study, climate extremes, topographic/geotechnical data, water balance, fiber route evidence, local code matrix and at least independent equipment quotations. The current memo supports a portfolio design discussion, not an investment commitment.
