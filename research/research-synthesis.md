# Research synthesis — from references to an original facility toolkit

**Date:** 3 September 2026. **Audience:** engineering portfolio reviewers and contributors. **Scope:** public, building-only 5 MW concept in a hypothetical tropical Indonesia setting.

The practical architecture is a small original calculation core with explicit interfaces to higher-fidelity tools. No reviewed source supplies investment assumptions, complete physical design, validated operations and local approvals as one portable package. The source families complement one another; they do not collectively establish construction readiness.

The [DOE/NREL design guide](https://datacenters.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/2025-07/best-practice-guide-data-center-design.pdf) supports organizing the project around environmental, cooling, electrical and energy boundaries. [DOE's water guidance](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/cooling-water-efficiency-opportunities-federal-data-centers) supplies consistent annual PUE and direct-site WUE definitions. The project's COP curve, tropical profile and costs are original illustrative assumptions.

[Uptime's certification overview](https://connect.uptimeinstitute.com/tier-certification) makes maintenance objectives and certification distinct. Therefore the project carries an explicit maintenance matrix and records common-path gaps rather than inferring a Tier from N+1 arithmetic. [ASHRAE's resource directory](https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/ai-data-center-framework/tools-standards-and-resources) points to updated Datacom material; the 2021 thermal guide is recorded as a historical edition, with equipment-specific/current guidance still required.

[OCP liquid-distribution guidance](https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-acf-reference-design-guidance-white-paper-pdf-1) informs the FWS/TCS interface checklist. The project draws its own loop diagram. The [OCP colocation guide](https://www.opencompute.org/documents/colo-facility-guidelines-for-ocp-racks-v40) is versioned to 2020 and Open Rack v1/v2; its accessibility does not make it a current product specification.

[NVIDIA DSX](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Omniverse-blueprints/omniverse-dsx-blueprint-for-ai-factories) informs digital-twin interoperability, while [MathWorks Simscape](https://github.com/simscape/Data-Center-Simscape) informs disturbance and power/thermal comparison cases. Their licenses, assets and dependencies differ. Neither runtime was executed. [Schneider RD100](https://www.se.com/us/en/download/document/RD100DSR0_EN/) provides architecture context from its accessible official record; the underlying PDF was inaccessible. [TradeOff Tools](https://www.se.com/sg/en/work/solutions/data-centers-and-networks/trade-off-tools/) supplies comparison categories, not open equations or verified output parity.

The [SustainDC paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07841) motivates considering weather, cooling and storage interactions while exposing reduced-order limits. This release uses a separate original balance model, not a validated reimplementation of SustainDC. [NIST HB 135e2025](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/hb/2025/NIST.HB.135e2025.pdf) informs discounting, replacements and residual accounting; its US program rates and old example prices are not adopted.

Research stops at a defensible concept architecture: definitions and reference scopes have primary support, while unavailable source content, local evidence and simulation validation are listed as open gaps. Continuing broad searches would not supply a real site, manufacturer data or design sign-off. The [source register](source-register.md) preserves dates, scope and access limits; the [gap matrix](evidence-gaps.csv) records unresolved decisions.
