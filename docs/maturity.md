# Maturity and release gates

| Discipline | v0.1 evidence | Explicit next gate |
|---|---|---|
| Investment | Reproducible pre-tax cash flow and sensitivity | Market evidence, tax/debt/construction model, investment review |
| Requirements | OPR, assumption register, traceability | Signed owner acceptance |
| Site | Synthetic gated scoring and due-diligence specification | Utility offers, survey, geotechnical and flood evidence |
| Electrical | Capacity/battery math, concept SLD, N+1 tests | Fault/arc-flash/protection/earthing study and coordinated drawings |
| Mechanical | Heat/flow/plant math and loop concept | Vendor curves, hydraulics, psychrometrics and ride-through study |
| Geometry | Original SVG/DXF and rack-only USD massing | Surveyed site, coordinated BIM, structural/egress coordination |
| CFD | Bulk airflow reference plus validation protocol | Mesh/solver convergence and measured boundary validation |
| Controls | Six offline supervisory cases; points and narrative | Reviewed cause/effect, hardware-in-loop and vendor approval |
| DCIM/twin | Matching IDs, geometry and synthetic telemetry | Historian integration and commissioned sensor calibration |
| Reliability | FMEA and simple common-cause model | Fault tree, maintainability demonstration, real failure data |
| Sustainability | Direct-site water and operational grid carbon | Meter data, embodied carbon and refrigerant/diesel inventory |
| Commissioning | FAT/SAT/IST plans and software evidence | Witnessed physical tests and signed acceptance |
| Operations | SOP/MOP/EOP framework, maintenance schedule | Site-specific approval, drills and handover training |

No row claims construction readiness. All local law, jurisdiction, climatic design extremes, commercial prices and site conditions remain unresolved until substantiated.

## Contribution milestones

**v0.2 — calibrated inputs:** EPW import with time-zone/leap validation; real tariff structure; equipment part-load curves; reproducible comparison against independent software.

**v0.3 — coordinated design:** IFC/BIM authoring, site massing, protection studies, hydraulic model and validated CFD case. Each integration needs solver version, input digest, convergence evidence and deviations.

**v0.4 — lifecycle finance and operations:** construction draw schedule, debt/tax/working-capital options, event-based reliability and read-only historian connector with data-quality tests.
