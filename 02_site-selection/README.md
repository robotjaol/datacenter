# Site selection

The executable ranking uses `data/site-candidates.csv`: five 1–5 scores, weights 35% grid, 25% hazards, 15% water, 15% fiber and 10% access. Scores are 20 × weighted average. All entries are fictional. Grid and hazard eligibility gates take precedence over ranking; failed candidates remain visible at the bottom.

## Due-diligence request

Obtain utility point of connection, firm/import limits, load-growth provisions, substation/common-corridor mapping, fault level, supply quality, lead time and interconnection cost. Record documentary evidence, not distance-to-line assumptions.

Obtain flood surface elevations and climate projections, drainage/outfall constraints, topographic survey, geotechnical investigation, seismic basis, contaminant/corrosion exposure and local weather extremes. Confirm land title, setbacks, access/turning envelope and emergency response routes.

For water, establish source reliability, permitted volumes, quality, wastewater requirements and drought restrictions. For fiber, confirm distinct duct/building entrances and route independence with carrier evidence. The absence of water or fiber evidence cannot be compensated by a high grid score.

Repeat ranking under alternative weights and preserve rejected candidates plus rationale. No actual Indonesia location is recommended by this dataset.
