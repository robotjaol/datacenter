# Sustainability boundaries

The model reports gross energy, grid imports, optional on-site PV use, operational grid emissions and direct-site water. Grid emissions = imported kWh × assumed kgCO2e/kWh / 1,000. Direct water = IT kWh × assumed L/kWh / 1,000. The factors are illustrative and constant; neither is a verified Indonesian measurement.

PUE remains based on gross facility consumption even when PV reduces grid imports. Direct-site WUE excludes upstream power-generation water. Cooling is closed-loop with air-cooled heat rejection; the small water factor is a miscellaneous direct-water allowance, not an evaporative-tower model. It does not count recirculated flow as water consumption.

A lifecycle assessment requires a functional unit and horizon, building/material quantities, equipment embodied factors, replacements, transport, refrigerant leakage, generator fuel, operational electricity and water, and end-of-life scenarios. Record geographic/temporal factor representativeness and allocation rules.

No renewable-energy certificate, market-based accounting claim, net-zero claim, diesel emissions inventory, embodied carbon result or cooling-water savings guarantee is made by v0.1. The project exposes these boundaries so future contributors can extend them without double counting.
