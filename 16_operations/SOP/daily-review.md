# SOP-001 — Routine condition review template

Purpose: organize an operator review without changing plant state. Required inputs: current approved topology, shift handover, alarm/event record, asset status, maintenance schedule, capacity report and instrumentation quality.

1. Reconcile active alarms, inhibited alarms and open work permits with the handover log.
2. Confirm data age, missing samples, clock offsets and implausible meter deltas before interpreting trends.
3. Compare demand with the capacity available under current isolations; flag simultaneous work that removes required redundancy.
4. Review cooling and environmental trends against approved equipment-specific limits; record excursions and escalation.
5. Record exceptions, owner, priority, next action and timestamp. The next shift acknowledges outstanding items.

Evidence: dated review, named reviewer, referenced measurements and disposition of every critical exception. Thresholds and escalation contacts remain site-specific approved inputs.
