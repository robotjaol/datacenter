# Operations handbook

Operational ownership begins at design review. Maintain current asset registers, approved procedures, training records, condition data, alarm settings and configuration backups. Reconcile each work order with affected capacity and prohibited concurrent work.

Use the SOP for routine review, MOP template for planned changes and EOP template for abnormal events. The templates require site-specific details and authorization before physical use. No live equipment sequence is encoded here.

## Read-only dashboard specification

Show facility/IT energy and PUE over matching intervals; A/B supply status and data age; available versus committed capacity; cooling temperatures/flows/quality; leak and high-priority alarms; open maintenance isolations; battery status; and unresolved commissioning issues. Display measurement quality and missing intervals prominently.

The workbook's Overview tab is the portfolio dashboard. It is a scenario snapshot with editable financial/sizing cells and clearly imported energy evidence; it is not a connected operations screen. Do not use synthetic telemetry for dispatch decisions.
