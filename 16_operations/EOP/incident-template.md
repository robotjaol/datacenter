# EOP-001 — Incident coordination template

Triggers include supply loss, thermal excursion, leak, fire/life-safety event and loss of trusted telemetry. Use the site's approved emergency arrangements and equipment-specific procedures. This template is a coordination record, not a live intervention guide.

Record the event time, affected assets, confirmed observations, data quality, service impact and immediate site authority. Protect personnel under the approved emergency plan. Assign an incident lead, communications owner and evidence recorder. Do not infer safe equipment status from a stale dashboard.

Track decisions, permissions, escalation, permitted actions and stop conditions against the approved response. Preserve original alarms and logs. Restoration requires verified prerequisites and formal release; close with timeline, root-cause review, corrective actions and procedure/training updates.
