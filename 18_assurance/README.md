# Assurance, safety and jurisdiction review

The project has no real site or authority determination. The code register names review topics and evidence owners rather than asserting that an international standard applies automatically in Indonesia. Qualified local review must determine applicable editions, permits and authorities.

Review land/building use, structure and seismic basis, fire compartments and evacuation, electrical safety/earthing/protection, battery chemistry and hazard controls, generator fuel/emissions/noise, refrigerants, water and discharge, occupational safety and physical security. Record decisions with exact legal/standard source, edition and applicability rationale.

Controls/security boundary: separate supervisory monitoring from local protection and safety functions; manage least privilege, remote access, backups and change logging. Define recovery from network or time-service loss. All portfolio examples are offline and contain no credentials or equipment endpoints.

The highest-risk engineering gaps are common-path failures, cooling ride-through, structural/rack loads, protection coordination and fire/life safety. Track them to owners and evidence; do not conceal them behind an N+1 label or an attractive layout.
